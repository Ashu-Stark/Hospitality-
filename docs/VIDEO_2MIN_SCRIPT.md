# HOI product film: "One Saturday" (2:00)

**For:** the voice artist, the screen recorder and the video editor.
**Idea:** follow one hotel through one busy Saturday, from a bad 6 a.m. to a calm evening. Every feature appears as a moment in that
day, not as a list. 16 shots, about 265 words of voice-over at the pace of the current video (about 2.4 words per second), with room
for the screens to breathe.
**Rule:** every shot uses a real screen from the live apps (accounts in `credentials.local.md`; run `python scripts/wake_demo.py` first).

---

## The look and sound

| | Choice |
|---|---|
| Frame | 1920×1080, 30 fps. Off-white background (#F7F7FA) with soft blue-violet glows from the HOI logo. |
| Devices | Real screen recordings inside thin dark device frames: one laptop (owner), two phones (guest, staff). Soft shadow, no reflections. |
| Camera | Slow push-ins of 3–5% per shot; one smooth zoom onto the single thing that matters. No spins, no bounces. Cuts land on the voice. |
| Type | One clean sans-serif. Captions are 2–5 words, bottom left, the key word in HOI blue. They appear 0.2 s after the voice says it. |
| Taps | Hide the pointer; add a soft white tap ring and a light tick sound on every tap. |
| Music | Soft piano and warm pad. Quiet and slightly tense for 0:00–0:14, lifts at the logo (0:14), steady pulse through the product, resolves on the last line. −18 dB under the voice. |
| Voice | Warm, unhurried Indian English. The Hindi line in shot 8 is said by a second voice (a staff member), clearly and naturally. |

### Act 1 · The bad morning (0:00–0:18)

**Shot 2 · 0:04–0:10 · Everything at once**

  1. "HS07 · Sorry, fever, can't come today"

- **Sound:** music holds one tense chord.

**Shot 4 · 0:14–0:18 · Reveal**

- **Caption:** *Meet HOI*

- **Sound:** music lifts; soft whoosh.

### Act 2 · The day, handled (0:18–1:05)

**Shot 6 · 0:27–0:33 · Room 104**

- **Caption:** *Scan. Tap. Done.*

**Shot 7 · 0:33–0:43 · The right person**

- **Caption:** *The right person, on shift.*

**Shot 8 · 0:43–0:50 · Just speak**

- **Caption:** *Just speak.*

**Shot 9 · 0:50–0:59 · 11 a.m., checkout**

- **Caption:** *Checkout → clean → ready.*
- **VO:** "Eleven a.m. A guest checks out, and the cleaning job is created by itself, with photo proof before the room is ready again."

**Shot 10 · 0:59–1:05 · A guest's afternoon**

## Accuracy check before export

- **Staff:** shown only as IDs (HS05) and role icons, never names or faces. The shot 2 messages are illustrative.
- **Leaving risk:** "may be thinking of leaving", never "will quit".
- **Forecast numbers:** shot 14 is the 1-day-ahead result on the public Portuguese hotel dataset (10.9 vs 1.9 rooms). Keep the source line on screen.
- **Features to leave out:** no money, revenue, energy or booking-system features.
- **Hindi:** the Hindi on screen is a draft; have a native speaker check the frames shown.
