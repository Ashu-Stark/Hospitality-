Straight answers

Questions hotels ask

Does HOI predict who will quit?

No. It ranks who may need support this week and explains why (for example sustained overtime, no weekend off, a pay gap). Flags exist to offer help, never to punish or decide dismissals. Every reason shows how strongly the data supports it.

What employee data do you need?

Employee ID, department, skills, contract type, shifts, leave and task records. Names, gender, religion, caste, health and family status are not collected, and staff imports with such columns are rejected.

How are before/after photos verified?

Photos are taken in the app's live camera, stamped with the server's time (never the phone's), tied to the job and worker, and hashed with SHA-256 so later edits are detectable. This cannot stop someone photographing a screen; a code shown at capture time raises the bar.

Is it proven on real hotels?

Not yet. Everything shown runs on a realistic synthetic hotel. Accuracy numbers are reported beside simple baselines, and the next step is a pilot with a real property.

Can each hotel group keep its own data?

Yes. Each group is a separate workspace with its own properties, staff, jobs, inventory and data source, and can point to its own backend.
