Straight answers

Questions hotels ask

Does HOI say who will quit?

No. It shows who may need a friendly chat this week, and why (for example a lot of overtime, no weekend off, or lower pay). It is there to help people, never to punish or fire anyone.

What staff details do you need?

Staff ID, department, skills, contract type, shifts, leave and jobs. We never take names, gender, religion, caste, health or family details. Staff lists with these are not accepted.

How are before and after photos checked?

Photos are taken in the app, not picked from the gallery. Our server adds the time (not the phone), the job and the person, and can tell if a photo was changed later. It cannot stop a photo of a screen.

Has it been tested in a real hotel?

Not yet. The demo uses a made-up practice hotel. The next step is a trial with a real hotel.

Is each hotel's data kept separate?

Yes. Each hotel has its own staff, jobs, stock and data, and can use its own server.
